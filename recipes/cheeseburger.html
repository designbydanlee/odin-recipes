Cheeseburger

This crowd-pleasing classic American cheeseburger is bound to take your grilling session to the next level. Meat, cheese, and spices, can't get better than that.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb. ground beef
  • 8 slices of American cheese
  • 3 tbsp of McCormick Gourmet Burger seasoning
  • 1 package of Broiche burger buns
  • Condiments: Ketchup, mustard, mayo
  • Optional: Chopped lettuce

Directions

  1. Form ground beef into 4 patties.
  2. Season patties according to preference.
  3. Start grill and add patties when grill reaches 350 degrees.
  4. Cook burgers to preference, add butter to broiche bun tops, place on grill.
  5. Place cheese onto patties, remove broiche bun tops after cooked to preference.
  6. Place burger onto broiche bun bottoms.
  7. Add condiments and toppings as preferred.
