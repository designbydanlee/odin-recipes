Tacos

These easy crowd-pleasing street tacos are back with simple but bold flavor.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb of ground beef
  • 1 whole yellow onion
  • Chopped cilantro
  • Chili powder
  • Garlic powder
  • Sea salt
  • 12 pack of flour tortillas

Directions

  1. Heat skillet to medium and add ground beef.
  2. Add Chili powder, garlic powder, and sea salt.
  3. Chop and dice yellow onion.
  4. Add yellow onion to ground beef.
  5. Stir and smash ground beef to preferred chunkiness.
  6. Warm flour tortillas via microwave or other preferred method.
  7. Add ground beef mix to tortillas.
  8. Chop cilantro and add as garnish.
